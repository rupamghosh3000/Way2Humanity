# Way2Humanity — Product Decisions Still Required

These items are intentionally identified instead of silently invented.

## 1. Operating Jurisdiction

The initial launch country must be confirmed. The current product presentation references India-specific CSR/regulatory concepts, but final legal requirements must be confirmed before launch.

## 2. Beneficiary Verification

Decide whether beneficiaries are:
- self-reported
- verified by Helper
- verified by NGO
- verified by platform
- verified by multiple parties

## 3. Emergency Cases

Define whether the platform:
- only supports community assistance
- supports emergency reports
- redirects emergencies to official services

The platform must not imply that it replaces emergency services unless formally designed and authorized to do so.

## 4. Donation Eligibility

Define which mission categories may accept public donations.

## 5. Human Verification Network

Define who can become a Verifier and what evidence/identity checks are required.

## 6. Reputation Policy

Define exact point values and penalties transparently before production.

## 7. Public Evidence

Define which before/after evidence can be publicly displayed and what consent is required.

## 8. CSR Verification

Define legal/business verification requirements for corporate accounts.

## 9. Fees

The presentation proposes a 3–5% platform fee on fundraising. The final fee policy, tax treatment, payment-provider charges, refunds, and disclosure wording must be finalized before production.

## 10. Blockchain / Humanity Tokens

The presentation lists blockchain-based Humanity Tokens as a future phase. Keep this out of the core MVP until the regulatory, technical, and product rationale is defined.

## 11. Payment Geography

The MVP payment provider and supported currencies must be finalized.

## 12. Messaging

Decide whether Helpers and Seekers communicate:
- through platform chat
- masked contact
- phone
- WhatsApp
- no direct communication

## 13. Location Privacy

Define how exact coordinates are handled for:
- public missions
- Helpers
- admins
- donors
- sensitive cases
